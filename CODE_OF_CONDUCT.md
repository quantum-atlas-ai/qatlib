# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in the
Qatlib community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and scholarly formalization community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

* Focusing on mathematical precision, constructive technical feedback, and
  faithful representation of source literature
* Respecting differing mathematical modeling viewpoints and design trade-offs
* Accepting responsibility for mistakes, misformalisations, or oversights, and
  correcting them transparently
* Properly attributing upstream mathematical libraries, formalizations, and
  research papers

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

* Personal attacks, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments
* Public or private harassment
* Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
* Misrepresenting unproven definitions or vacuous models as proved theorems

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported to the project maintainers via GitHub Security Advisories or repository
contact channels. All complaints will be reviewed promptly and fairly.
